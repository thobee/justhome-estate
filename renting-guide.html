Plan for more than monthly rent

Decide what you can comfortably spend before you shortlist homes. The catalog currently displays monthly NGN prices, but the owner or agent must confirm the actual rental period and payment terms.

  • Ask about deposits and any agency or legal fees.
  • Check service charges and the cost of water, power, internet, and parking.
  • Allow room for moving costs and any furnishings you need.
  • Request a written breakdown rather than assuming everything is included.

Choose an area around your everyday

Think about how you travel to work, school, shops, and the places you visit often. Visit the surrounding streets and check transport access at the times you would usually travel.

Bring a simple viewing checklist

A photograph cannot answer every question. Walk through the property and check its condition in person.

  • Test taps, water pressure, doors, windows, and available electrical fittings.
  • Look for damp patches, leaks, and unfinished repairs.
  • Ask how power and water are supplied and billed.
  • Check parking, building access, shared facilities, and security arrangements.
  • Confirm the rooms and facilities shown in the listing are included.
  • Note the work the owner agrees to finish before move-in.

Ask the questions that affect your decision

Who manages the property? When is it available? What is the agreed rental period? Who handles repairs? What payments are due, and what documentation will you receive?

Confirm the owner's or agent's identity, inspect the home, and get the terms in writing before you arrange payment. A viewing request on JustHome is not a confirmed reservation.

Keep your shortlist and take the next step

Save homes you like to your account. For an available published listing, send a preferred viewing date and wait for confirmation from the agent. Homes marked Sample listing are examples and cannot be booked.
