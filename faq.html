Help with your home search

JustHome questions, answered

Understand listings, accounts, saved homes, prices, and viewing requests before you take the next step.

Can I browse homes without an account?

Yes. Search homes, compare prices, and open property details without signing in. Create an account to save your shortlist, send an enquiry, or request a viewing.

What does the listed price include?

The current catalog displays rent in Nigerian naira per month. Ask the listing agent to confirm the rental period, deposit, service charges, agency fees, and any other costs before committing.

How do I arrange a viewing?

Open an available published property, sign in, and submit your preferred date. Your request is not confirmed until the agent agrees the appointment details.

Are sample apartments available to rent?

No. Homes marked Sample listing are examples you can explore and save. You can contact JustHome about similar homes, but a sample cannot be booked.

Can I pay for a property through JustHome?

The current viewing flow does not collect rental payments. Inspect the home and confirm the owner's or agent's identity and rental terms before arranging any payment.

Where can I find my saved homes?

Sign in and open My account. Your shortlist and viewing requests are linked to your account, so you can return to them later.

Still have a question?

Include the property name and area so we can understand your enquiry.
